# Local SEO Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
**Target:** Google Business Profile (Google Maps)
**Goal:** Dominate the "Local 3-Pack" for searches like *homestay in dehradun*, *luxury stay dehradun*, *3bhk penthouse dehradun*.

Google Maps is the highest-converting organic channel for hospitality. To win, your profile needs two things: **Velocity of Reviews** and **Profile Activity**.

---

## 1. The "Review Engine" (Harvesting 5-Star Reviews)
You must systematically ask every happy guest for a review. Timing is critical.

### The Checkout Message (Send via WhatsApp 2 hours after checkout)
*Why 2 hours? They are likely at the airport or in a cab on the way home, with nothing to do and still riding the high of a good vacation.*

> "Hi [Name]! Thank you so much for choosing ValleyGaze Penthouse. We absolutely loved hosting you. Safe travels back home! ✈️
>
> As a small local business, Google reviews mean the absolute world to us. If you enjoyed the views and your stay, would you mind taking 60 seconds to drop us a 5-star rating? 
> 
> [INSERT_DIRECT_GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK_HERE]
> 
> Hope to host you again next time you're in Doon! ⛰️"

### Responding to Reviews (Crucial for SEO)
Google's algorithm reads your *responses*. Do not just say "Thank you". Sneak your keywords into your replies naturally.
* **Guest says:** "Loved the stay, beautiful views."
* **You reply:** "Thank you [Name]! We are so glad you enjoyed the mountain views from our **3BHK penthouse**. It was a pleasure hosting you here in **Dehradun**. Come back for another **workcation** soon!"

---

## 2. Weekly Profile Activity (Google Posts)
Google rewards businesses that use their platform actively. Post an update to your Google Business Profile 1-2 times per week. Treat it like Instagram, but for SEO.

### Post Archetype A: The "Atmosphere" Post
* **Photo:** A high-quality shot of the balcony, a cup of coffee, or the mountain view.
* **Caption:** "Monsoon season is hitting Dehradun and the valley views from the penthouse have never looked better. The perfect weather to sit back with a coffee. ☕️ Book your peaceful getaway today. #DehradunStay #LuxuryHomestay"
* **Button:** 'Book Now' (Link to your website)

### Post Archetype B: The "Workcation" Post
* **Photo:** A laptop on the desk or dining table with the view in the background.
* **Caption:** "Tired of working from the same four walls? Our 3BHK penthouse offers high-speed WiFi and panoramic mountain views. Upgrade your WFH setup to a Work-From-Mountains setup. 💻⛰️"
* **Button:** 'Learn More' (Link to your website)

### Post Archetype C: The "Proximity" Post
* **Photo:** A shot of Mussoorie or a local Dehradun cafe.
* **Caption:** "Planning a trip to Mussoorie? Stay away from the extreme traffic! ValleyGaze is located perfectly in Dehradun, giving you a luxury basecamp just a 45-minute drive from Mussoorie mall road. 🚗"
* **Button:** 'Book Now' (Link to your website)

## 3. The Photo Upload Strategy
Don't just upload photos directly from your phone with names like `IMG_9384.JPG`. Google reads file names.

1. **Rename Files Before Uploading:**
   - BAD: `image_1.jpg`
   - GOOD: `luxury-homestay-dehradun-living-room.jpg`
   - GOOD: `3bhk-penthouse-dehradun-balcony-view.jpg`
2. **User-Generated Content:** Whenever a guest tags you in a beautiful Instagram story, screenshot it (with permission) and upload it to the Google Business Profile as an "Owner" photo. Raw, real photos convert incredibly well.
